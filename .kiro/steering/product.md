# FinShield AI
AI-powered security, privacy and compliance monitoring for an Indian digital lender (NBFC).
Mandatory demo: a privileged account exports unusual volumes of KYC documents -> one CRITICAL alert
with a sealed evidence trail, AI explanation, controlled containment and regulator deadlines.
Platform must Monitor, Detect, Correlate, Alert, Investigate, Align with compliance, Respond, Visualize.
Priority risks: KYC exposure, unauthorized financial data access, compromised privileged credentials,
abnormal transactions, transfers to unauthorized destinations, consent/purpose violations, cloud misconfig.
This is a hackathon PoC: working end to end beats perfect. Never leave a stub that breaks the demo path.
